OUR PROGRAMS

- PLAYGROUP (1.5 – 2.5 yrs): Tiny tots take their very first steps away from home, into a world of songs, soft toys and warm caregivers.

- NURSERY (2.5 – 3.5 yrs): Curious explorers learn to share, listen and make their very first friendships.

- LKG (3.5 – 4.5 yrs): Letters, numbers, colors and shapes — discovered through hands-on activities, not worksheets.

- UKG (4.5 - 5.5 yrs): Confident little learners get ready for big school — with strong basics and even stronger self-belief.
